3. **TreeHouse Villas Koh Yao** — forested northern tip and resort-run boat transfers; **no children under 16**. https://www.treehouse-villas.com/ and https://www.treehouse-villas.com/koh-yao/contact
4. **9 Hornbills Tented Camp** — adults-only hillside tented villas with individual infinity pools and buggy to beach facilities at Koyao Island Resort. https://9hornbills.com/ and https://koyao.com/9hornbills

**Geography not to confuse:** Tha Khao (limestone views, east), Pasai (beach and independent cafés), remote north (TreeHouse boat logistics), hillsides and tented camp, western village roads and sunset piers. Neither Phuket nor Krabi photos may be reused as island images. Arrivals depend on boat operators, piers and weather; no fixed duration or pricing promised.
