## Editorial positioning

Phu Quoc is not a single beachfront product. The editorial distinction is between the polished southwest coast at Bai Truong, the theatrical Bill Bensley property on Khem Beach, family-scale private-pool villas at Ong Doi Cape and an intimate French-Indochine heritage-style boutique retreat near Duong Dong. A premium guide should make these choices clear before comparing nightly rates.

## Hotel shortlist: operating official properties

1. **Regent Phu Quoc** — official https://www.ihg.com/regent/hotels/us/en/phu-quoc/pqcrr/hoteldetail — western coast, Duong To/Bai Truong. Suites and villas; ocean sunsets. Official IHG describes airport as around 15 minutes by car (traffic-dependent). Distinguish hotel suite, private-pool villa and branded real-estate residences; do not promise one category's facilities for another.
2. **JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay Resort & Spa** — official https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/pqcjw-jw-marriott-phu-quoc-emerald-bay-resort-and-spa/overview/ — Khem Beach, south. Bill Bensley university-inspired design; rooms and suites as well as selected villas with plunge pools. Do not claim every room has a private pool or a particular beach view.
3. **Premier Village Phu Quoc Resort — Managed by Accor** — official https://www.premiervillage-phuquoc.com/ — Ong Doi Cape at southern tip. Official operator states 215 villas, each with a private pool, but garden/ocean/beachfront access and multi-bedroom configurations must not be conflated. Peninsular setting and two beaches, not a city-centre hotel.
4. **La Veranda Resort Phu Quoc — MGallery Collection** — official https://mgallery.accor.com/en/hotels/6479.html and https://laverandaresorts.com/ — Duong Dong Beach / Tran Hung Dao. 74 rooms and suites in heritage-style Indochine mansion setting, botanical gardens and beach. Communal resort swimming pool; don't imply every accommodation has a private pool.

## Areas / travel practicalities

- Bai Truong / Duong To: western-coast sunsets, resort atmosphere and airport access.
- Khem / Bai Kem: southern beach, JW Marriott, access towards An Thoi.
- Ong Doi Cape / An Thoi: multi-bedroom private-pool villas, coves and boat logistics.
- Duong Dong / Tran Hung Dao: market, shops, restaurants and the boutique La Veranda.
- Northern national park / Ganh Dau: forest and nature trips; separate long drive from southern hotels.

Vietnam National Authority of Tourism official guide https://vietnam.travel/place-to-go/mekong-delta/phu-quoc/ identifies the Phu Quoc National Park, Sao Beach, Hon Thom cable car, Duong Dong night market, pepper and fish sauce traditions, international airport and the generally drier November–April season. Wet-season swells may close water excursions; check the precise date. Air transfers and ferry/boat operations are not guaranteed.
