# Four-language SEO release gate

88 native Arabic and Russian destination names and 34 country labels are explicitly defined, independent of machine-translation output. Locale routes are stable /ar/travel/... and /ru/travel/...; Arabic uses Modern Standard Arabic (not a single-country locale), server-rendered lang/dir and mirrored gradients. Each guide and hotels page has its own title, description, self-canonical, image social metadata, inLanguage, and localized breadcrumb/FAQ data. The global sitemap and HTML hreflang share one approval gate. French/English approvals previously contained only the last nine guides (18 destination and 18 hotel entries); those existing approvals are retained, not silently extended to unreviewed pages. Desaru Coast has manually authored RU/AR translations; both variants returned HTTP 200 with exact supplier IDs and approved photos in preview. Remaining machine-generated drafts are not automatically approved. Google indexing is not guaranteed by sitemap inclusion.
