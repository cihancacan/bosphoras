# Russian and Arabic travel rollout

The 88 original FR/EN guides remain the authoritative source. Translation files are static and keep hotel names/IDs and image paths untouched. A translated guide is rendered only if its locale data exists. All locales stay noindex until individual editorial and rendering review. Desaru Coast is the initial full translation. Arabic is RTL. Exact booking links retain the verified English supplier locale until Arabic/Russian supplier interface support is confirmed. No new paid translation provider is configured.
